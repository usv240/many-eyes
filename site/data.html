Bring your own votes.

Have a crowd, a classroom or a mapping event label anything as destroyed, intact or unsure. Upload the votes here and get back which labels are validated and which should go to experts. It runs the same engine as the stream, entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Or use your own file

A CSV with a header row. Required columns: tile_id, user_id and vote. Optional: truth, lon and lat.

  • truth marks a hidden check. Write the known answer, destroyed or intact, on any row of that tile.
  • lon and lat add the tile to the GeoJSON download, so results open in any map tool.
  • Up to 5,000 tiles and 200,000 votes per run.
